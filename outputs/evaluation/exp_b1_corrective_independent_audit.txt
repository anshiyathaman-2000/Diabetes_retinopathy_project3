      "Model Prediction on IDRiD_064: Class 2 (Moderate NPDR) with 41.5% confidence."

      The actual prediction record in outputs/predictions/resnet50_exp_b1_corrective_res512_official_predictions.csv
      for IDRiD_064 is:
      - Ground Truth: Grade 3
      - Predicted Class: Grade 2 (Moderate NPDR)
      - Exact Confidence: 0.534460 (53.45%)
      - Class Probabilities:
        * No DR (Grade 0)        : 0.133837 (13.38%)
        * Mild NPDR (Grade 1)    : 0.179630 (17.96%)
        * Moderate NPDR (Grade 2): 0.534460 (53.45%)
        * Severe NPDR (Grade 3)  : 0.112460 (11.25%)
        * PDR (Grade 4)          : 0.039614 ( 3.96%)
    * Root Cause: In the original contaminated Exp B1 run (resnet50_exp_b1_res512_official_predictions.csv),
      the confidence on IDRiD_064 was indeed 0.414918 (41.5%), with 23.26% assigned to Grade 0 due to the
      conflicting training gradient of IDRiD_118. When writing the report, the author inadvertently copied
      the contaminated model's confidence value instead of referencing the corrective prediction file.
    * Scientific Significance: The corrective model's probability assigned to Grade 0 actually dropped
      from 23.26% to 13.38% (a -9.88 percentage point drop), demonstrating that removing IDRiD_118 (Grade 0)
      successfully stopped the model from leaning toward No DR for this fundus pattern.
  - Anomaly 2: Selective Metric Framing in Executive Summary
    * The report highlights gains in QWK (+0.0160) and ROC-AUC (+0.0304) when comparing Corrective vs. Contaminated Exp B1.
    * Audit Finding: While QWK and ROC-AUC did improve, raw Test Accuracy declined from 59.22% to 55.34%
      (-3.88 percentage points, 4 fewer samples correct), and Macro-F1 shifted from 0.4333 to 0.4203 (-0.0130).
      A balanced research paper must transparently present both the accuracy decrease and the ordinal kappa increase.

SECTION 6: RESEARCH SUITABILITY & UNRESOLVED LIMITATIONS
====================================================================================================
Suitability for Research Paper: SUITABLE FOR PUBLICATION
The Exp B1 Corrective experiment satisfies all rigorous peer-review standards:
1. Data Isolation Integrity: All train-test and train-val cross-split duplication has been eliminated.
2. Standardized Official Holdout: Results on the official 103-sample test benchmark allow fair,
   uncompromised comparison against published benchmarks.
3. Sensitivity Analysis Compliance: Secondary reporting on the 102-sample deduplicated test set proves
   that metric conclusions are not sensitive to the presence of IDRiD_064 (QWK 0.6272 vs 0.6282).
4. Reproducibility: Full config, seed (42), split manifest, training curves, checkpoint weights, and
   prediction CSVs are preserved on disk.

Unresolved Limitations to Explicitly State in the Paper:
1. Mild NPDR (Grade 1) Class Collapse:
   Zero recall (0/5) in official test and 0/4 in validation demonstrates that resolution scaling alone
   cannot overcome severe minority class imbalance. Specialized objective functions (e.g., Focal Loss,
   asymmetric margin loss) or targeted oversampling are required.
2. Intra-Partition Clinical Grading Discrepancies:
   Two duplicate pairs in IDRiD possess divergent clinician annotations:
   - Train: IDRiD_028 (Grade 4) vs. IDRiD_327 (Grade 3)
   - Val  : IDRiD_021 (Grade 1) vs. IDRiD_391 (Grade 2)
   The paper should disclose this clinical label noise as an inherent characteristic of the IDRiD dataset.
3. Single Seed Run:
   Exp B1 Corrective was executed with seed 42. While results are reproducible, full multi-seed variance
   (e.g., 5-fold cross-validation or seeds 42, 43, 44) will strengthen statistical claims in subsequent phases.
4. Model Diversity at 512x512:
   Only ResNet-50 has been trained at 512x512 under this corrective protocol. Hybrid architectures
   (CNN-Transformer, CNN-BiLSTM) remain to be evaluated at this resolution.
