[CRITICAL ANALYSIS] Overfitting Assessment:
The claim that "overfitting was absent" in earlier preliminary notes requires qualification:
1. At Epoch 6 (Selected Checkpoint):
   - Train Loss: 0.8778 | Val Loss: 0.9119
   - Train Acc : 69.70% | Val Acc : 68.67% (Minimal generalization gap on validation)
   - Train QWK : 0.8357 | Val QWK : 0.8606
2. From Epoch 7 to Epoch 13 (Subsequent Overfitting Phase):
   - Train Loss dropped from 0.8778 down to 0.5663.
   - Train Acc rose from 69.70% up to 82.12%.
   - Train QWK rose from 0.8357 up to 0.9070.
   - Val Loss remained stagnant (0.9119 -> 0.9154, reaching 0.9556 at Epoch 10).
   - Val QWK degraded to ~0.76 - 0.82.
   Overfitting clearly began after Epoch 6. Early stopping functioned correctly by preventing
   the selection of overfit late-epoch weights.
3. Train/Val vs. Holdout Test Gap:
   - Val Acc = 68.67% vs. Test Acc = 59.22% (-9.45% gap)
   - Val QWK = 0.8606 vs. Test QWK = 0.6112 (-0.2494 gap)
   This ~9.5% accuracy drop and ~0.25 QWK drop between validation and test reflects domain shift
   inherent to IDRiD train/test splits, exacerbated by the small validation sample size (N=83).
