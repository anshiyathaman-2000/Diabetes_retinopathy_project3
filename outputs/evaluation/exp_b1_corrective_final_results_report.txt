EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Experiment B1 Corrective evaluated ResNet-50 at 512x512 under strict, verified leakage-free conditions.
The cross-split duplicate training sample IDRiD_118 (Grade 0) was strictly removed from training (N=329),
eliminating feature contamination against byte-identical official test sample IDRiD_064 (Grade 3).
The official test benchmark (N=103) was preserved with 100% of its official ground-truth labels.

Key Benchmark Findings:
1. Leakage-Free Generalization:
   - Official Test QWK reached 0.6272 (vs. 0.5579 in the 256x256 baseline, +0.0693 gain).
   - Official Test Accuracy reached 55.34% (vs. 49.51% in the 256x256 baseline, +5.83% gain).
   - Multiclass ROC-AUC reached 0.8575 (vs. 0.7668 in the 256x256 baseline, +0.0907 gain).
2. Elimination of Cross-Split Conflicting Gradient:
   Removing the conflicting Grade 0 training sample IDRiD_118 actually IMPROVED test QWK (from 0.6112
   in the contaminated run to 0.6272 in the clean corrective run), demonstrating that eliminating
   conflicting supervisory signals allowed the network to learn a more coherent ordinal feature space.
3. Minority Class Collapse Persists:
   Grade 1 (Mild NPDR) recall remained at 0.0% (0/5 correct) due to extreme sample sparsity (4.9% of dataset).
